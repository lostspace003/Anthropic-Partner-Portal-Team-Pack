Owner and Admin Action Guide

What the firm must do as a Certified partner · How to do it · Which portal document has the full detail

Your job in one paragraph

Keep us in good standing, move us from Certified to Select, and make sure the team does the few things that earn credit: certify people, register delivery work, and publish one customer story. Everything else in the portal is either a how-to for those three things or a benefit that unlocks later.

1. The rules that matter (Program Guide, Version 2, effective 5 Oct 2026)

Tier Certified individuals Deployed joint customers Public customer stories
Certified (us) 3 or more – –
Select (next) 10 or more 2 or more 1 or more
Preferred 100 or more 15 or more 3 or more
Global Premier 1,000 or more 100 or more, across 3 regions 15 or more

  • All requirements must be met together, measured over the last 365 days.

  • Only these exams count: Claude Certified Developer – Foundations, Architect – Foundations, Architect – Professional. The Associate exam does not count. One person counts once even with several certifications.

  • Deployed joint customer: a unique customer with a live Claude production deployment where we are the registered delivery partner. Pilots and proofs of concept do not count. We must give Anthropic the customer’s Organization ID (or AWS / Google Cloud / Azure account ID).

  • Public customer story: live on a public URL (no NDA or login), names Claude and a third-party client, describes a production deployment, shows at least one measurable business result, and the client approved it.

  • Reviews: Certified and Select are promoted monthly (starts November 2026) – meet the requirement by month end and the promotion follows the next month. Preferred and Global Premier are reviewed 1 January and 1 July (from 1 January 2027). Once promoted we keep the tier through at least the next annual review (31 December) plus a 90-day remediation window.

  • Moving up more than one tier is allowed if we meet the higher tier’s requirements.

3. What we may say publicly about our tier

Do: use the approved line – “[Firm] is Certified in the Claude Partner Network.” Wait for the promotion email first. Post on social media only. Use the badge from the Hub.

Don’t: say “Anthropic-certified” or “certified by Anthropic”; call ourselves Select, Preferred or Global Premier; issue press releases or newswire items; quote tier requirements, incentive amounts or comparisons with other partners; name customers without their consent.

7. Know before you rely on it

  • We have no named Anthropic partner manager (that starts at Global Premier; Preferred gets a pooled team). Some decks assume one – our route is PAM AI, then Partner Operations.

  • Co-sell and deal referral start at Preferred. Read the Sales guide for how to sell on our own.

  • Several documents have stale dates or conflicting figures; see Start Here, section 6.

  • A Delivery Readiness Designation (DRD) exists for firms with 10 or more active Developer / Architect certified people, 3 documented deployments and 1 contactable customer reference. Certified is the minimum tier named for DRD engagement (contact Partner Operations). Plan for it after Select.
