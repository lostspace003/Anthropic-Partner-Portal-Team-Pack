The one rule to remember

Only Developer and Architect certifications count toward our tier. The Associate exam does not. We have 4 certified people today and need 10 for Select – so 6 more people should take a Developer or Architect exam.

1. The four exams

Exam Level List price Counts toward tier? Best for
Claude Certified Associate Foundations $99 No Foundational knowledge; useful for non-engineering roles
Claude Certified Developer Foundations $125 Yes People who build with Claude
Claude Certified Architect Foundations $125 Yes People who design solutions; a natural first exam for architects
Claude Certified Architect Professional $175 Yes Advanced architect level

  • Price: the Training and Certification Guidebook lists these prices; the Program Guide shows a 100% exam discount through 31 December 2026, and our own booking showed Free at checkout. Book before 31 December 2026. The discount applies automatically – there is no code to enter.

  • Format: 120 minutes, scenario-based, scored 100–1,000, 720 to pass; delivered by Pearson VUE online (proctored) or at a test center; results are immediate.

  • Validity: a certification is valid 12 months; renewal is a free non-proctored assessment on the Partner Academy.

  • Retakes: wait 14 days after the first attempt, 30 after the second, 90 after the third; maximum four attempts per exam per rolling 12 months.

  • Eligibility: exams are for people at accepted Claude Partner Network firms, registering with their company-domain email. No prerequisites; prep courses are free and recommended.

  • One person counts once toward tier, even with several certifications. Certifications must be current and show in the Partner Hub (updates weekly).

4. Beyond tier: the Delivery Readiness Designation (DRD)

DRD helps Anthropic match us to customer opportunities. It asks for: 10 or more active Developer / Architect certified people, three documented deployments (Claude Code or Cowork), one contactable customer reference, and evidence across six areas (environment and deployment, enablement, governance and administration, adoption and change management, troubleshooting and escalation, security posture). Certified is the minimum tier named; completing enablement does not automatically award it. Use the portal’s “04 Practice self-check” every 30 days and email Partner Operations when ready.
