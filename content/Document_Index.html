How to use this index

Find the document by its portal name, read the one-line description, check the Team and Note columns. “Select+” means the content applies only from Select tier upward – we are Certified. “CONFIDENTIAL” means the document is stamped confidential – keep it inside the firm. Before sending any document to a customer, confirm Anthropic’s sharing terms.
