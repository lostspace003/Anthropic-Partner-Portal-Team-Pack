How we sell Claude as a Certified partner · What Anthropic will and will not do for us · Which portal documents to use

The most important fact for sales

Co-sell with Anthropic starts at the Preferred tier. At Certified, Anthropic’s own co-sell deck tells us to “plan to lead your own sales motion”. We cannot file Deal Referrals (Preferred and above), so we do not get deal protection or Anthropic account-executive involvement through the program.

What we can do: sell and deliver our own services, and register the delivery work (Services Registration) – that earns full credit toward tier at every level.

“01 The co-sell motion”, p.3 – what Anthropic brings to a deal at each tier. Deal Referral is “No” until Preferred.

Pitch frame and guardrails (Positioning and sizing)

• Sell one of three outcomes: smarter employees, faster processes, or transformative products.

• Land small, prove, then expand: a two-to-four-week pilot with metrics agreed first.

• Never quote price, discount, seat minimum, term or funding – “Anthropic will quote that”. Take all rates from the current public pricing page.

• Pilot burn is not ARR – size consumption from real pilot traffic, with a low / base / high range.

Scoping toolkit, p.3 – the five tools in the order you use them: (1) qualifying question, (2) value conversation, (3) scoping worksheet, (4) pilot exit criteria, (5) nine-item checklist through go-live.

2. Five habits from Anthropic’s co-sell playbook that still help us

These come from “01 The co-sell motion” (p.11–18). The deck assumes referrals, so use them as our own internal sales discipline and confirm with Partner Operations before relying on Anthropic involvement.

  • Let the customer ask for Anthropic’s involvement – pull, not push.

  • Five Checks before involving Anthropic on a deal: (1) a named sponsor who owns a budget and knows you are involving Anthropic; (2) a workload described in one sentence and tied to one of three outcomes; (3) the customer agreed a next step and the engagement is scoped (what, for whom, how long); (4) a realistic first phase sized to the users in scope; (5) our own team uses Claude every day.

  • One-page brief instead of a long deck (see the Partner Opportunity Brief template).

  • 20-minute first call with a fixed script (0–3 / 3–6 / 6–15 / 15–20 minutes). The worked example uses 15 minutes – pick one and standardise.

  • Fortnightly update email to everyone on the deal.

Watch-outs before quoting

• The Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 documents say other 5.5 models are “coming soon” – they are now released.

• The “which model” tables in some 2-pagers still show older models (Haiku 4.5, Sonnet 5, Opus 5).

• The Opus 4.8 deck gives a benchmark (SWE-bench Pro 64.3%) that conflicts with the 2-pager (69.2%) – use the 2-pager.

• The Developer Platform deck is dated (mid-2025 market data). Always re-check live pricing.

5. Industry evidence packs – only where we have real proof

Eight blank forms (Financial services, Healthcare and life sciences, Industrial manufacturing, Retail and consumer, Technology/media/telecom, Travel/transportation/hospitality, Security (CISO), Office of the CFO). Complete one only if we have delivered work in that industry: rate our capability honestly, choose 2–3 use cases, get customer permission, record a short demo, return it to Anthropic. Read “02 Industry buildbooks” first. Starter buildbooks are visible to Select and above only.

6. Customer stories sell for us

  • A public story is also a tier requirement for Select – sales should ask for it at kickoff, not at the end.

  • Two paths: (1) a live public story that counts toward tier; (2) a marketing submission for Anthropic to produce and promote. See “06 Customer Stories with Anthropic – Two Paths”.

  • A story needs a real number (for example hours saved or cost reduced) – “improved efficiency” is declined.

7. Later, when we reach Select or Preferred

  • Select: partner directory listing – customers can find us and send inquiries (“Referrals from the Partner Directory”, “Building a great directory profile”). Pre-draft our profile now: name, website, short description, logo, city, “accepting new clients”.

  • Preferred: Deal Referral (90-day deal protection), co-sell, the Partner Opportunity Brief to the Anthropic partner manager, the worked co-sell example (“04 A worked co-sell example”).
