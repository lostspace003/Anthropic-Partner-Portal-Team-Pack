6. Read this before you trust a document

  • Several documents disagree. The agreement outranks the Program Guide; the Program Guide outranks the FAQ and the Hub decks. When two documents differ, follow the Program Guide (Version 2, effective 5 October 2026).

  • Two support emails appear: partner-support@anthropic.com (Program Guide and newer documents) and partner-success@anthropic.com (older Hub decks). Start with PAM AI in the Partner Hub; if you email, use partner-support first.

  • Review dates differ: older decks say 15 January; the Program Guide says 1 January and 1 July for Preferred and Global Premier, annual review 31 December, and monthly promotions for Certified and Select starting November 2026.

  • Some pages are out of date: the Customer Story Guide’s “October 1, 2026” deadline has passed; several model sheets still list older models; some decks pre-date the Certified tier.

  • Many decks are stamped CONFIDENTIAL (product decks, co-sell series, training guidebook). Treat the whole pack as internal. Do not forward to customers unless Anthropic’s terms say you may.
