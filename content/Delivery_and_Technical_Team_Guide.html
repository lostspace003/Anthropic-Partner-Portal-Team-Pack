Delivery and Technical Team Guide

How we deliver Claude rollouts and get credit for them · What to register · Which portal documents to use for each job

Two jobs for the delivery team

1. Register every Claude engagement in the Partner Hub so it counts as a deployed joint customer (we need 2 for Select).

2. Deliver it the way Anthropic’s playbooks describe – 90 days, three gates, measurable adoption and value – so the result is strong enough to become a public customer story.

1. Register delivery work (Services Registration)

Open to every tier. This is how Anthropic knows we delivered a customer’s Claude deployment. Credit starts at stage 4 (Delivery Started) or later and counts once the deployment reaches sustained production use. Pilots and proofs of concept do not count.

Services Registration Guidebook, p.1 – what a Services Registration is and how it works.

Step by step

☐ When: register at scoping, and no later than 60 days after delivery work begins. Anthropic reviews within five business days.

☐ Where: Partner Hub (partnerhub.anthropic.com) > Services > Submit Project. One registration per customer use case.

☐ Fill in: customer company; a decision maker with signing authority and a work email (no info@ or contact@); our delivery lead; start and end dates; Claude products; a short description of the use case.

☐ Organization ID: required for every service type except pure training or advisory. Claude.ai customers: claude.ai > Settings > Account > Organization ID. API customers: platform.claude.com > Settings > Organization. Customers on Bedrock, Vertex AI or Foundry give their AWS (12-digit), Google Cloud or Azure account ID. Without it the registration is rejected.

☐ SOW: optional, but it moves you to the front of the queue; required if the work is funding-eligible (pricing may be redacted).

☐ Keep the stage current: 1 Scoping, 2 Proposal, 3 SOW confirmed, 4 Delivery started, 5 Delivery completed. Pending and stages 1–3 do not count yet.

☐ Answer any “missing information” request by the date given or the registration is declined (you may resubmit).

☐ Check status: Hub dashboard (Current Tier panel) refreshes weekly. Status is Pending, Approved or Rejected.

“03 Registering and working deals”, p.9 – what a complete registration needs (customer, contact, products, Organization ID).

If two partners claim the same customer: Anthropic settles delivery-lead conflicts within 10 business days, in this order of evidence: customer attestation, contracted scope, delivery activity, registration order.

2. The delivery method in one picture

Anthropic’s method has four steps (“Activating Claude”): Day One → Stand-up → Department Activation → Enterprise Rollout, run over 90 days with three gates.

“03 The 90-day rollout map”, p.3 – days 0–30 lay the foundations, 30–60 activate, 60–90 prove and decide.

Gate When Evidence
Wave one opens Day 30 First cohort of 50–100 users across two to four whole teams; day-0 baseline recorded; about 60% weekly active and 20% beyond chat
Adoption checkpoint About day 60 About 70% weekly active and 35% beyond chat (example calibration – agree your own targets up front)
Readout and scale decision About day 90 Value against baseline, adoption and spend by week, three wins. One decision: scale, extend, adjust or stop. The customer’s sponsor presents it

4. First-account checklist (do these in order)

☐ Register the engagement (Hub > Services) and collect the customer’s Organization ID.

☐ Book the customer’s platform, security and identity teams for Day One; run the Claude Code checklist items 1–4 first if Claude Code is in scope.

☐ Capture the day-0 baseline (hold first access until it is on file) and have finance sign the value target.

☐ Fill the adoption plan with nine named roles (first four by kickoff).

☐ Run the entry checklist; agree adoption targets and gates in writing.

☐ Every cohort gets the “101” within five business days of provisioning; admins trained two to three weeks before launch; 1–3 connectors live by end of week one.

☐ Send the two-weekly update (T13) and use PAM AI for escalations (data retention, encryption keys, capacity, model issues).

☐ At day 90 the sponsor presents the readout; update the Services Registration to the right stage.

☐ Ask the customer for a public story with a measurable result – tell the owner and marketing.

7. Caveats in the delivery documents

  • Prices and beta features: the Playbook’s rate card was last verified 18 August 2026 – re-check every price, model name and beta feature before quoting.

  • Small inconsistencies: exit checklist has 6 items in one document and 8 in others; adoption targets are 60% in some places and 70% in others; the Workshop set’s week-4 row looks like a copy error. Agree one version with the customer and write it down.

  • Tier: nothing in the delivery documents is gated to a tier except references to a named Partner Success Lead and funded engagements – confirm with PAM AI.
